Questions

Frequently asked

Does it need internet to sell?

No. Selling, stock and reports all work offline. Internet is used to activate or buy the licence, for M-Pesa prompts, off-site backups and updates.

How many computers can I use?

One licence covers the shop's main computer and as many station computers as you like on the same network. A second branch needs its own licence.

Where is my data kept?

On your main computer, in your shop. RG Devs never receives your sales, stock, customer or staff records. Backups are encrypted with your own password. See the privacy policy.

What happens when my licence runs out?

You get a reminder a week before. When it ends, the till pauses until you buy more time — your data is kept, and it switches back on as soon as you pay.

Can I take M-Pesa at the till?

Yes. Type the M-Pesa code from the customer's message, or connect your own Safaricom Daraja keys to send the payment prompt to the customer's phone.

How do updates work?

The app downloads updates by itself and asks before installing, so it never interrupts a sale.
