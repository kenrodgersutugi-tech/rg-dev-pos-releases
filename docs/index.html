Reports

Know your day at a glance

  • Sales, cash and M-Pesa by day — cash figure matches the drawer.
  • Profit estimate using real cost of goods, minus expenses.
  • Top items, sales by staff member, and a controls report of voids, refunds and discounts.
  • Export PDF: a branded A4 business report with your logo, ready to print, email or file.
Business report

One click to a report you can hand over

  • Any period: today, last week, last month or your own dates.
  • Your shop's name, details and logo at the top; profit & loss with percentages; daily sales chart.
  • Prepared-by and signature lines, page numbers. Saved straight to a PDF file on the main computer.
Receipts

Receipts that look like your shop

  • Upload your logo — it is turned into sharp black-and-white dots for thermal printers.
  • Opening hours, a return policy, your Buy Goods Till or Paybill number, a QR code.
  • Each computer keeps its own paper width; the main computer prints without the print window. Reprints say COPY.
Barcodes

Scan, label, sell

  • Any USB barcode scanner — scan wherever the cursor is. A carton's barcode sells a carton, a single's sells one.
  • Unknown barcode? It beeps, and a manager adds it to the product right there.
  • Print shelf labels with name, price and barcode; after a price change, reprint only the labels that changed.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does it need internet to sell?

No. Selling, stock and reports all work offline. Internet is used to activate or buy the licence, for M-Pesa prompts, off-site backups and updates.

How many computers can I use?

One licence covers the shop's main computer and as many station computers as you like on the same network. A second branch needs its own licence.

Where is my data kept?

On your main computer, in your shop. RG Devs never receives your sales, stock, customer or staff records. Backups are encrypted with your own password. See the privacy policy.

What happens when my licence runs out?

You get a reminder a week before. When it ends, the till pauses until you buy more time — your data is kept, and it switches back on as soon as you pay.

Can I take M-Pesa at the till?

Yes. Type the M-Pesa code from the customer's message, or connect your own Safaricom Daraja keys to send the payment prompt to the customer's phone.

Which barcode scanners and label printers work?

Any USB barcode scanner that types like a keyboard (most do out of the box). Shelf labels print on standard A4 label sheets (24 or 40 per page) on any printer, or on a label printer with 50 × 30 mm or 40 × 25 mm rolls. Receipts work on 58 mm and 80 mm thermal printers.

Can I send my accountant a report?

Yes. Reports → choose the dates → Export PDF gives a branded A4 business report with sales, profit and loss, payment methods, staff and top items.

How do updates work?

The app downloads updates by itself and asks before installing, so it never interrupts a sale.
