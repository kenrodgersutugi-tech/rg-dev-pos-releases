Privacy policy

RG Devs Point of Sale (also called RG Dev POS) · Effective 12 November 2026 · Replaces the version of 14 October 2026

The short version. Your shop's records — sales, stock, customers, staff, patients — stay on your own computer. RG Devs receives only what is needed to run your licence and, if you buy in the app, to take the M-Pesa payment. We never see your business records, passwords or backups, and there is no advertising or tracking.

1. Who we are

RG Devs is a software developer based in Meru, Kenya, and makes RG Devs Point of Sale (“the system”). For the information described in section 4, RG Devs is the data controller. Contact: rgdev.ke@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your computer

Everything your shop records is stored on the shop's main computer, in C:\ProgramData\RG Dev POS: sales and receipts, stock, customers and credit accounts, staff accounts and the audit log, patient records if you use the healthcare add-on, your own M-Pesa (Daraja) keys (encrypted), product pictures, local backups and logs. Other computers in the shop read this data over your local network; it is not sent to RG Devs. Uninstalling the app keeps this folder on purpose; Settings → About → Remove RG Dev POS data from this computer deletes it.

3. Your shop's responsibilities

For the customer, staff and patient information you enter, your shop is the data controller under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. You decide what to record, who has access, and how long to keep it.

4. What RG Devs receives

For your licence (activation, renewals, trial)

  • The licence key (we store only a one-way hash of it).
  • This computer's ID — a one-way code made from the Windows installation ID and the disk serial number (the raw values never leave the computer).
  • The computer's name and the app version.
  • For a free trial: the same, without a key. We keep the computer ID so each computer gets one trial.
  • A record of activations, renewals and refusals, with their dates.

When you buy a licence in the app

  • The M-Pesa phone number you type, used once to send the payment prompt through Safaricom. We store it only masked (for example 0712•••678).
  • Your shop's name (to name the licence), the plan, the amount, the M-Pesa receipt code and the result of the payment.

When you buy directly from RG Devs

The business name and the contact person's name, phone and email, for the licence and receipts.

For M-Pesa prompts sent by your shop

If your shop connects its own Daraja keys, payment prompts go from your computer directly to Safaricom. Safaricom's confirmation comes back through an RG Devs relay, which stores the request ID, result, M-Pesa receipt code, amount and a masked phone number, and deletes them after 7 days.

What we never receive

Your sales, stock, customer, staff or patient records; your backups; passwords or PINs; your own M-Pesa keys.

6. Where our records are stored

Licence, purchase and relay records are stored with Neon (a database provider) in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany), protected in transit and at rest. Only RG Devs can access them.

7. Off-site backups

If you turn on off-site backup, an encrypted copy goes each day to a folder you choose — your Google Drive, OneDrive or a USB drive. It is encrypted with your backup password before it leaves the computer; RG Devs cannot open it, and a lost password cannot be recovered.

8. Other services the system uses

  • Safaricom — M-Pesa payments, under your or RG Devs' agreement with Safaricom.
  • Google or Microsoft — Google Drive or OneDrive, if you choose them for backups.
  • WhatsApp — only when you choose to share a receipt that way.
  • GitHub — downloads and updates of the Windows installer edition; it sees your computer's IP address.
  • Microsoft — installation and updates of the Microsoft Store edition, under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

These providers may keep short technical logs, such as IP addresses. We do not sell or share your information for marketing.

9. How long we keep it

  • M-Pesa relay records: 7 days.
  • A new licence key waiting to be collected by the buying computer: at most 7 days.
  • Licence, trial and purchase records: while the licence exists, then as long as tax law requires for purchase records.
  • Your shop's own records: on your computer, until you delete them.

10. How the system protects data

Encrypted connections between your computers using your shop's own certificate; stations must be approved; passwords and PINs stored only as secure hashes; role-based permissions checked on the main computer; an audit log of sensitive actions; encrypted backups.

11. Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask to access, correct or delete information RG Devs holds about you, or object to its use. Email rgdev.ke@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (www.odpc.go.ke).

12. Children

The system is for businesses and is not directed at children.

13. Changes

We will post any change here with a new effective date, and tell licensed shops about important changes in the app or by message.
